# 0.9.0 — English reading assistant

Date: 2026-10-28. Package `org.watchersarchive.app`, versionName `0.9.0`, versionCode `14`.

## User interaction

Open a comic and tap blank page space to show the controls. “英文助手” enables local English OCR on the current displayed page; it defaults off for a new reading session. Tap a recognized word to show the bottom card. Long-press a word, or choose “本行短语” in its card, to select up to twelve words from the same recognized line. This is a user-selected text span, not automatic idiom or bubble detection. Turning the page recognizes the new current page and drops the previous selection. Closing the assistant cancels its lookup and removes all underlines and highlights.

The bottom card shows the selected original text, available dictionary phonetic notation, Chinese meaning and part of speech, original OCR line, and optional device-generated reference translation of that line. Missing data is explicitly marked. “发音” uses the installed Android English text-to-speech engine, preferring an available offline voice. It never autoplays, and stops on card close, page change, disabling, Activity backgrounding and disposal. A missing system voice is explained instead of claiming speech works.

The OCR bitmap, layout and zoom all remain in the existing reader. The main image gesture recognizer handles word taps and long presses; the visual word overlay does not intercept input. Swipes and pinch gestures starting over a word therefore retain their normal behavior. Hit testing and decoration share the exact Fit letterboxing, centre-origin scale and translation math. Double taps zoom without issuing two dictionary lookups. Blank fitted margins retain ordinary toolbar taps.

## Dictionary and contextual help

Word definitions are read from a licensed bundled ECDICT dataset, with source attribution and the upstream MIT license included. Indexed gzip shards avoid loading the whole dictionary into memory. Lookup is local and does not require an account, the user's PC or an external dictionary API. An earlier public dictionary service returned HTTP403 during normal checks and is not used as a default dependency.

Contextual reference translation and unknown phrases are optional ML Kit English-to-Chinese operations on the phone. The first language-model download requires an explicit card action and defaults to Wi-Fi, with a cellular choice. The card explains its approximately 30MB model storage. The SDK download can continue if the panel is dismissed; the app cancels page work and suppresses late results. Normal dictionary meanings do not require that model. Human names, artistic fonts, OCR errors, slang and ambiguous senses can still be wrong or absent. Common senses and a reference line translation are not a claim to professionally explain every comic's meaning.

There is no vocabulary-save button, vocabulary database, page upload or modification of comic artwork. This release does not connect paid cloud translation or implement Chinese relettering.

## Resource and lifecycle boundaries

- Recognition uses at most a 4.1MP displayed bitmap, 60 text blocks, 512 word elements and 16,000 word characters. It keeps image coordinates and line identity. OCR results are cached as text/rectangles for at most sixteen pages and 160,000 counted characters; cached pages do not retain Bitmaps.
- ML Kit native OCR is serialised until its SDK task actually completes. Cancelling a coroutine releases its continuation reference and prevents old-page publication without allowing an unbounded queue of native bitmap jobs.
- Separate page and selection revisions guard page changes, concurrent taps, dismiss, disable and close. A late or noncooperative OCR/dictionary result cannot replace a newer page or selected word.
- A dictionary bucket is bounded by decompressed bytes and record count, with a four-bucket memory LRU and bounded word results. Its bundled app resources and explicitly downloaded language models are separate from the fixed 500,000,000-byte disposable comic cache.
- Existing Range validation, ETag, page CRC, pixel limits, download leases, book files, personal book names, progress and bookmarks remain protected.
